I've always been a people-watcher. I notice why someone orders the same drink every time, or why a "limited time" sign makes us walk a little faster. Psychology gave me the questions, and data science gave me a way to answer them. I spent two years at Princeton running social and behavioral research, and now I'm pointing that same curiosity at marketing.

The part I love most is the messy middle: taking a pile of survey responses or a very long spreadsheet and finding the one insight a team can actually act on. I'm looking for marketing and consumer insights roles where understanding people is the job. If that sounds like your team, I'd love to chat at [hacindy02@gmail.com](mailto:hacindy02@gmail.com) or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hacindy02/).
