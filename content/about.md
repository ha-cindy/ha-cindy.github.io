I'm a master's student in **Marketing Analytics and Insights** at Rutgers Business School (expected May 2027). Before that, I graduated from Northwestern University in 3 years with a double major in **Data Science and Psychology** (2024).

From 2024 to 2026, I was a lab manager at Princeton University in the Department of Psychology, working with [**Dr. Anuj Shah**](https://anujkshah.com) and [**Dr. Betsy Levy Paluck**](http://www.betsylevypaluck.com/). I built NLP and ETL pipelines in Python, ran social network analysis on data from 500+ business owners, and presented my work at the Association for Psychological Science conference in 2025.

My work sits at the intersection of **behavioral science and data**. I'm interested in how people make decisions, form judgments, and move through the world, and I use quantitative methods to study it. These days I'm applying that to marketing: segmentation, positioning, customer journeys, and lifecycle strategy. You can see it in my [Duolingo portfolio](/portfolio/duolingo-aim/).

I'm looking for roles in **marketing insights or consumer insights** where I can bring a people-first lens to data problems.
