In AI in Marketing (AIM), each of us picked one brand and built on it all semester. I picked Duolingo, an app I've used on and off since 2014 and currently use for Chinese, Vietnamese, and Spanish.

Each assignment builds on the last. I start by finding where Duolingo can grow, decide what it should own, then write, design, and plan for it. Every deck was researched and drafted with AI tools, and each case study notes which ones I used and how.
